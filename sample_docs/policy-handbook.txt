# Northstar Community Center Policy Handbook — SYNTHETIC DEMO

This is fictional demonstration data created for EvidenceDesk. It is not a real policy.

## Building access
The community center opens at 8:00 AM on weekdays. The building closes at 9:00 PM Monday through Friday. Weekend hours are 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Visitors must sign in at the front desk. Staff badges must be visible inside the building. The west entrance is accessible by ramp. The public parking lot has 42 spaces. Bicycle racks are located beside the north entrance. The reception desk is on the ground floor. The building has three public floors.

## Room reservations
Meeting rooms may be reserved up to 60 days in advance. A standard room reservation lasts no more than two hours. Community groups receive room use at no charge. Reservations must be cancelled at least 24 hours before the start time. The largest meeting room holds 30 people. The small study room holds 4 people. Room keys are collected from reception. Food is allowed only in the conference room. The projector is stored in meeting room B. Events with more than 20 attendees require staff approval.

## Program registration
Program registration opens on the first day of each month. Residents receive priority when a class is full. A participant may join up to three classes per season. The center offers six seasonal sessions each year. Class cancellations are sent by email. Participants under age 12 must attend with an adult. The swimming lesson program is offered in summer. The art studio is closed on Mondays. A waitlist is used when a class reaches capacity. Refund requests must be submitted within seven days of registration.

## Equipment and services
The lending library allows two items per member. Items may be borrowed for 14 days. A late return fee is one dollar per day. The community garden has 18 plots. Garden plot applications are due by March 1. The computer lab contains 12 workstations. Public computer sessions are limited to one hour. Printing costs ten cents per black-and-white page. The center provides free public Wi-Fi. Lost property is held for 30 days. The first aid kit is kept at the front desk.
